Privacy Policy for "UltraLink" (omoshorts)

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. About the app

"UltraLink" is a personal desktop application used by a single individual (the developer) to publish and schedule their own short videos on their own TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Instagram accounts. The app is not offered to the public.

2. Data we access

When the owner connects an account, the app may access: basic profile information (to confirm which account is connected), the list of the owner's Pinterest boards, and the ability to upload and publish videos to the owner's accounts. The app does not access the data of any other users.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's own computer. We do not operate any servers and do not collect, transmit, sell or share any data with third parties. Video files are sent directly from the owner's computer to the selected platforms.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes the app's local files. Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform under connected apps.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page.
