UltraLink by omoshorts

UltraLink is a personal desktop application for Windows. It lets its owner select a short video on their own computer, write a caption, and publish or schedule it to the owner's own YouTube, Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

The app is not offered to the public and is used only by its developer with their own accounts.
